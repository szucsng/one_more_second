## Generated assets

The following are original, procedurally generated assets included in this repository:

- `assets/sounds/*.wav` — synthesized in `tools/generate_audio.py`
- `assets/music/neon_drive.ogg` — synthesized in `tools/generate_audio.py`
- `assets/icons/*.svg`, generated PNGs — original vector design
- `assets/textures/splash.png` — generated from `splash.svg`
- `assets/theme/ui_theme.tres` — generated by `tools/build_theme.gd`
- All in-game vector drawing (ships, obstacles, backgrounds, particles) — original
  GDScript canvas rendering.

No third-party sound samples, music, textures, icon packs, paid asset-store content or
trademarked characters are used.
